# Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & Market Structure Core Foundations

## 1. Market Structure
- **Break of Structure (BOS)**: Trend continuation signal. In an uptrend, price creates a higher high with a decisive candle close above previous swing high. In a downtrend, a candle close below previous swing low.
- **Change of Character (CHOCH)**: First signal of potential trend reversal. Occurs when price breaks the internal swing point that led to the recent extreme high or low.

## 2. Liquidity Concepts
- **Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)**: Stop-losses of short sellers located above swing highs and equal highs (EQH).
- **Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)**: Stop-losses of buyers located below swing lows and equal lows (EQL).
- **Liquidity Sweep**: A rapid price movement through liquidity pools to trigger stops and induce retail orders, followed by immediate rejection back inside the range.

## 3. Imbalances & Order Blocks
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG)**: A 3-candle imbalance pattern where the wick of candle 1 does not overlap the wick of candle 3, representing inefficient institutional delivery.
- **Order Block (OB)**: The last opposing candle before an aggressive expansion that breaks structure (BOS/CHOCH) and creates an FVG.
- **Premium vs Discount**: Above 50% equilibrium of a dealing range is premium (seek shorts); below 50% is discount (seek longs).
